# Trenchless Pipe Rehabilitation Resources

## Knowledge

- [NASSCO: Technologies Defined in the Pipeline Rehabilitation Matrix (2025)](https://nassco.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Technologies-Defined-in-Pipe-Rehabilitation-Matrix-2025-1.pdf)
  The single most authoritative source — NASSCO is the industry trade association that sets trenchless standards. Covers CIPP, SIPP, fold-and-form, and every other rehab method with vetted definitions. Use for: settling any terminology dispute; the primary source to cite in front of a client.
- [NASSCO: CIPP Specification Guideline (2023)](https://nassco.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/NASSCO-CIPP-Specification-Guideline_2023-1.pdf)
  Use for: CIPP installation and cure-method detail (ambient, hot water, steam, UV/LED) beyond the overview level.
- [NASSCO: Folded Pipe Trenchless Lining Technologies](https://www.nassco.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/feb_2013.pdf)
  Use for: fold-and-form process detail — manufacturing, deformation shapes (H/U/C), reversion method.
- [Wikipedia: Cured-in-place pipe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cured-in-place_pipe)
  Neutral, well-cited overview. Use for: a fast CIPP refresher and a clean list of cure-method variants.
- [Wikipedia: Sprayed-in-place pipe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sprayed_in_place_pipe)
  Use for: a fast SIPP refresher, and confirming how it differs mechanically from CIPP (no tube, direct spray/spincast).
- [Trenchlesspedia: What is Fold & Form Lining?](https://trenchlesspedia.com/definition/2287/fold--form-lining)
  Glossary-style industry reference. Use for: a quick-check definition of fold-and-form terminology.
- [Trenchless Technology magazine: "Light Bright" — UV curing in the CIPP market](https://trenchlesstechnology.com/light-bright-ultraviolet-curing-continues-to-make-inroads-in-the-cipp-market/)
  Trade journal, not vendor-authored. Use for: why contractors are adopting UV cure, market context. Cross-check specific performance claims against the NASSCO spec guideline.
- [Trenchless Technology magazine: "How to Complete a Proper CIPP Install"](https://trenchlesstechnology.com/how-to-complete-a-proper-cipp-install/)
  Trade journal field walkthrough with photos. Use for: the six-step install sequence — clean/inspect, insert, calibrate, cure, reinstate laterals, trim/final inspection.
- [WaterWorld: Spray-in-Place Pipe Rehabilitation](https://www.waterworld.com/drinking-water-treatment/distribution/article/14071209/spray-in-place-pipe-rehabilitation)

- [Trenchlesspedia: The Basics of Spray-in-Place Pipe](https://trenchlesspedia.com/the-basics-of-spray-in-place-pipe/2/3517)

- [Trenchlesspedia: What is Lateral Reinstatement?](https://trenchlesspedia.com/definition/2896/lateral-reinstatement)
  Use for: confirming lateral reinstatement (robotic cutter reopening covered branch connections) applies across lining methods generally, not just CIPP.
- [NASTT: Fold and Form Lining glossary entry](https://nastt.org/glossary/fold-form-lining/)
  NASTT (North American Society for Trenchless Technology) is the field's other major professional body alongside NASSCO. Use for: authoritative fold-and-form definition.
- [PE100+: Close-Fit Lining — Fold and Form Lining](https://www.pe100plus.com/PE-Pipes/Technical-guidance/Trenchless/Methods/Pipe-Rehabilitation/Close-Fit-Lining-Fold-and-Form-Lining-i1314.html)
