# Mission: Trenchless Pipe Rehabilitation Technologies

## Why
You work in sales / estimating and need to talk confidently about trenchless pipe rehab methods — specifically SIPP, fold-and-form, CIPP, and UV-cured lining — with prospects and clients. You have no prior civil/pipe background, so this starts from zero.

## Success looks like
- You can explain each method end-to-end, in plain language: what goes into the pipe, how it becomes the new pipe wall, and what makes each one distinct.
- You can correctly place all four terms in the right family — including catching that "UV-cured lining" is a curing method *within* CIPP, not a fifth sibling technology, a mix-up that would undercut credibility in front of a client.
- You can use accurate terminology fluently, without needing to check notes mid-conversation.
- (Added after Lesson 5) You know the actual materials, equipment, and process parameters used for CIPP, SIPP, and fold-and-form — enough to read a contractor's spec sheet or proposal and recognize whether it's using the right ones, not just narrate the process at a high level.
- (Added after Lesson 8) For CIPP specifically, you know what drives quality/performance/cost at the process level (prep, wet-out, calibration, cure, testing, emissions) and can build a credible value proposition for a resin product aimed at contractors/engineers — a second audience beyond the original end-client sales conversation, worth tracking as a distinct (related) thread rather than folding silently into the original one.
- (Added after Lesson 11) You know the standards, regulations, and incentive/funding landscape for CIPP across the US, Canada, and Europe — enough to know which standard edition or certification actually applies where, and what regulatory factors (e.g. BABA domestic-sourcing rules, the EU's 2024 drinking-water positive lists) constrain a specific pitch, for both the end-client and resin-marketing threads above.
- (Added after Lesson 14) You understand resin chemistry at a formulation level for the resin-marketing thread specifically — cure-time drivers (promoter/inhibitor/initiator chemistry), the thickening-vs-curing distinction, and every property on a real spec sheet mapped to cost/performance across the full product lifecycle (transport, storage, manufacturing, onsite use, in-service life, end of life). This is a genuine step past the original "plain-language over structural calculation" constraint below — flagged here rather than silently overridden, since it's now closer to formulation chemistry than sales fluency.
- (Added after Lesson 25) You can dissect a real, complete bid document package end to end — choosing the CIPP type, checking a real resin against a real municipality's actual (not generic) material minimums, and reading the pay-item/sampling/warranty clauses that actually drive a quote. This is the practical convergence of every thread above: the sales/estimating mission, the resin-marketing thread, and the regulatory-standards thread, applied to one live procurement package (Regional Municipality of Peel) rather than kept separate.
- (Added after Lesson 30) You know chemical injection grouting as a fifth trenchless method — non-structural joint/crack sealing, chemically and procedurally distinct from CIPP/SIPP/fold-and-form — well enough to recognize when a defect belongs there instead of a full liner, even though it isn't a resin-marketing opportunity for the same product line.
- (Added after Lesson 31) You understand the general-conditions/payment layer underneath a real municipal contract — document precedence, statutory and warranty holdback mechanics, and change-order markup schedules — well enough to reason about a contractor's or supplier's cash-flow exposure on a real job, not just the technical liner requirements.

## Constraints
- No civil/construction background — lessons build from zero, no assumed jargon.
- Time-limited — lessons should be short and give one tangible win each, not exhaustive engineering detail.
- Sales context — prioritize plain-language process fluency over structural calculations.

## Out of scope
- Structural design calculations (wall thickness, ASTM load ratings).
- Hands-on field installation / operator certification.
- Full NASSCO PACP inspection-coding depth.
