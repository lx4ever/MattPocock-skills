Updates from the September 30 presentations (Lessons 77–78)

  • Capacity: CAMX deck says 200,000 t; the 0930 deck and the profile say 180,000 t — use 180,000.
  • Footprint: CAMX lists six bases including Mexico; the 0930 note says a Mexico partner will be commissioned at year-end. No Canadian production is claimed anywhere; a Canada contact exists.
  • Certifications are product-scoped: ISO is company management; UL, ASTM E84, EN 45545-2, Lloyd’s and drinking-water rows each cover named grades. MFE 707A is Class A only with 5% Sb₂O₃ added.
  • SGS report SHRT26000374R01 (Apr 2026): MFE 711, 21 CFR 177.2420 extractables, Pass (ND) — one grade, two simulants; not drinking-water or CIPP evidence, and not one of the grades on the deck’s “FDA approved” row (Lesson 79).
  • Catalogue check: of MERICAN 3314 / 3316 / 3300 only 3316 is listed at 3–5 minutes cure and demould; 3314 is 8–10 minutes.

Arithmetic in the lessons (assumptions flagged there)

  • Peel’s 2,046.8 m of lined pipe at an assumed 12 mm wall is about 75 m³ of liner, about 80 t at most, so resin for the whole job is a few containers (Lesson 74).
  • Domestic content under Toronto’s formula is (A + A + B) ÷ Bid Price (Lesson 64); Peel’s floor is 51% (Lesson 69).
