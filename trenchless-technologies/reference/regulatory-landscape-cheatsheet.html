The one thing to check before assuming compliance

In every region, "meets the standard" needs a follow-up question:

  • US: which edition of the ASTM standard — an outdated edition citation is a real signal, not a technicality.
  • Canada: which province, and whose spec their RFPs actually cite — there's no single answer to default to.
  • Europe: a resin approved under an old national scheme (WRAS, KTW, ACS) hasn't automatically cleared the new 2024 EU-wide positive list — that's a separate, newer bar.
