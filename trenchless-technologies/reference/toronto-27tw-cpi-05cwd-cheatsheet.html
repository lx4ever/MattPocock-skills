What a resin supplier must be able to supply

  1. Form E data for up to three systems (Styrene, Styrene Free, Reinforced): flexural strength and modulus, short term and long term.
  2. Five accredited-lab reports on field-installed samples from at least three earlier projects, per system.
  3. Certified originals from the manufacturer: infrared spectrograph of the catalyzed mix; cure temperature requirements; example resin volume and cure time calculations.
  4. Resin identity and ratio: manufacturer, type, ID number, resin-to-felt ratio, excess resin % per ASTM F1216.
  5. Country of origin for the Pricing Form and the Domestic Supply Chain Plan “CIPP Resin” line.

Open questions to put to the City or a contractor

  • Where are the numeric flexural minimums for Form E? (Not in the Part 3 text supplied.)
  • How is a mid-job styrene-to-non-styrene switch under the 100 m rule paid, given no segment is pre-flagged?
  • Can any Canadian processing of imported resin meet the “51% of total direct costs” test?
  • Which addenda exist, and did any change the repair list or Form F? (B.02.07’s node reference also disagrees between spec and sheet.)
