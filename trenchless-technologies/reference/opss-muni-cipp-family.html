OPSS.MUNI 443 / 444 / 445 — Quick Reference

One CIPP design foundation, three service contexts, one clean pattern of additions.

The shared core: all three specify ASTM F1216 X1 fully-deteriorated design, 50-year design life, safety factor 2, groundwater at surface, 6.9 MPa soil modulus, 2124 kg/m³ soil density, CSA S6 CL-625-ONT or AASHTO HL-93 live load, and 2% default host pipe ovality. Every difference below is layered on top of that shared foundation.

The sampling-rate pattern

30% → 100% → 20% isn't a simple "pressure pipe = more testing" rule — forcemains (100%) and watermains (20%) are both pressurized, yet sit at opposite ends. The likely reason: NSF 61 certification and mandatory disinfection already give watermain work a materials/safety control layer forcemain work doesn't have. That's a reasonable inference from the pattern, not something either document states outright — worth confirming with an Ontario-experienced engineer before repeating as settled fact.

The one thing every version shares

All three require design work sealed by a licensed Engineer, submitted 14 days before installation; a two-CCTV-inspection regime (before and after lining) per OPSS 409; the same 15% flat-plate sample de-rating; and the same infrared spectrograph resin-verification check against a manufacturer reference sample.
