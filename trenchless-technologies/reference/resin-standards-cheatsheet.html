Remember: the three fire tests use different specimens, flames and pass marks — one does not imply another. And a fire result belongs to a formulation, not to a product name.

UL 94 and thickness

A rating is tied to the tested thickness (commonly 0.7, 1.5 or 3.0 mm). Illustrative only: V-0 at 3.0 and 1.5 mm, V-1 at 0.8 mm means V-0 can be claimed at 1.5 mm, not for a 1.0 mm wall. Ask: rated at what thickness, and is that at or below the customer’s part? No thickness is shown with the V-0 claims for 3313-3, 3314, 3315 and 3316 in the notes.
