Lesson 72: Reading the Repair Summary: Access, Flow Data, and What Gets Paid

Off-road manholes, bridges and a railway, flow numbers the owner won’t guarantee, and pay items that adjust — or don’t.

Lesson 35 read a repair list line by line. Here the more interesting reading is the addenda: what bidders asked, and the answers that moved risk onto the contractor.

Access is part of the price

Almost every segment is off-road: manholes sit on Lower Cooksville Trail, Stonebrook Creek Trail or easements, reached by trail from Mississauga Valley Blvd, Whitchurch Mews or a parking lot. The repair summary’s description column adds hard constraints:

  • S11 (1050 mm) passes below the Central Parkway East bridge, whose low clearance means access should be planned to both manholes without using the bridge passage.
  • S13 (1050 mm, 105.68 m): the pedestrian bridge between manholes has restricted loading — foot access only. Addendum 2 revised drawings 84787-D and 84864-D for additional pedestrian bridge loading requirements.
  • S17 (900 mm): crosses under the CP Rail diversion bridge, with restricted clearance and trail width — limited equipment access only. Work inside railway boundaries cannot proceed until CPR requirements and approvals are in place (Special Provision 41.1, Schedule B in Appendix 5.33).
  • Several other segments note that a temporary access road is required.

All the work of the 32 lined segments is also tied to flow diversion at MH1793066 and, for most, to a numbered bypass phase (1A, 1B, 2A, 2B).

The flow data was the most-argued topic

IssueWhat the Region said
Seven manholes had no flow figuresNo monitoring data exist; the numbers supplied come from the Region’s hydraulic model, “for information purposes only”. The Contractor verifies flows and designs bypass at its own cost.
Bypass 1A at MH1786806Tender documents give a peak dry weather flow of 547 L/s; the bypass model shows 1,023 L/s — about double. The Region would not issue one definitive value: flows are “for tendering purposes only” and bidders must assess the data and design what they consider appropriate.
Modelled below observedAt eight manholes, modelled peak wet weather flow was lower than observed (for example SMH1789840: 33 L/s modelled, 118 L/s observed). The Region confirmed both: models use design storms, monitors use real rain.
Bypass plan reviewAddendum 3 confirms a 4-week review for the bypass pumping plan, not the 8-week default in Appendix 5.18.

The teaching point: when the owner will not fix a design flow, bypass sizing becomes the contractor’s risk, and two bidders can price it very differently while both complying. The same logic appeared in Toronto’s GIS-length risk (Lesson 55).

Pay items that move with field conditions

  • B-5 CIPP lining: per metre, with lengths taken from the chainage on the V3 post-lining CCTV; payment becomes due only after V3 video is received and accepted. About 33 sub-items (a through gg), one per segment — Addendum 2 shows this: bidders asked about “B-5 p)” for segment S36, and the Region then updated lengths to match the plans (S36 is 85.41 m in the revised summary).
  • C-7 liner thickness increase (provisional): Addendum 3 changed the wording from payment “by 1 mm increments for the entire length” to payment per mm of thickness increase per metre of CIPP installed. Compare Toronto’s 1.5 mm steps (Lesson 65).
  • C-2 g) Primary Coating System surface area adjustment (provisional): the repair summary’s manhole dimensions come from as-builts and inspections, are not field verified, and govern over the drawings for pricing. If field measurements differ by more than ±10%, the full difference is paid or deducted at the unit price; within 10%, nothing.
  • Manhole inverts: where bypass keeps the chamber dry, coat the whole structure including invert channels, overriding Appendix 5.16 section 16. For four manholes outside the bypass limits (MH1790234, MH1790233, MH1790202, MH1786803) stop the coating at the channel edge.
  • Manhole modifications to allow lining are incidental to the lining price with no separate payment.
