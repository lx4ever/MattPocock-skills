Lesson 62: The Second Toronto Tender at a Glance: 27TW-CPI-05CWD

The City of Toronto’s September 2026 CIPP tender is smaller than the 2025 one and mostly egg-shaped brick — and it adds rules that touch a resin supplier directly.

Lessons 51–55 took apart Toronto’s 2025 tender. This is a second, newer Toronto package, so before the detail lessons here is the map: what it is, how it differs, and where this workspace’s earlier findings still apply.

Compare Lesson 51’s 2025 contract: 305 line items, 200 Working Days, Etobicoke South, mostly circular sanitary/storm/combined pipe. This one is smaller, shorter, and almost three-quarters egg-shaped brick.

What stayed the same

The five-part package structure (Lesson 51), the Form E idea (Lesson 53), and the payment schedule. This spec’s Item 4.1.1-4 pays lining on the same 5% / 25% / 50% / 20% milestones Lesson 55 described — V1 CCTV accepted, pipe preparation and V2 accepted, liner installed and V3 accepted, then all QA/QC paperwork accepted.

What is new — the next four lessons

  1. Canadian suppliers only (Lesson 63): bids from trade-partner, non-American and US-based suppliers are deemed non-compliant.
  2. A Domestic Supply Chain Plan with “CIPP Resin” on its own line (Lesson 64): resin origin now feeds a scored 10% evaluation advantage.
  3. Egg-shaped design (Lesson 65): a different design method, a 3% deflection cap, and a Form C experience requirement for non-circular pipe.
  4. A resin-maker deliverables list (Lesson 66): Form E plus a manufacturer-certified submittal, and a styrene-free rule with no pre-flagged segments.

Lesson 67 then reads the repair list and contract terms the way Lesson 35 did for Peel.

Two caveats about the folder I read. (1) It holds no addenda — the 2025 package had four, so anything an addendum changed is not reflected here. (2) The repair-list workbook has two sheets and the first is hidden. The spec’s item numbers match the visible second sheet (for example Item B.04.19 is SL1446590 and B.01.04 is the brick-to-PVC transition), so the visible sheet is treated as current. The hidden sheet numbers those same sewers differently (SL1446590 is B.01.01 there).
