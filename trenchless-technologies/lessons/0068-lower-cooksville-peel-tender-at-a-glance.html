Lesson 68: Peel Region’s Lower Cooksville Tender at a Glance: 2026-323T

A prequalified-bidders-only Peel tender for 32 lined segments of sanitary trunk sewer along Cooksville Creek, with dates that moved twice in the addenda.

Lessons 26–37 worked through an earlier Peel package. This is a newer one, from the same owner and mostly the same standard specifications, but a different project and a different procurement design.

The contract at a glance

WarrantyTwo years from substantial completion

Unlike Toronto’s open tender (Lesson 62), Peel ran a prequalification first. A prequalified general contractor must also carry one prequalified CIPP specialty subcontractor in its price: Aqua Tech, Capital Sewer, Insituform or Michels. Clearway is prequalified as a general contractor and for maintenance-hole rehabilitation but is not on the CIPP list. Lesson 61 profiled Capital Sewer; it appears here as both a general contractor and a CIPP specialist.

What is being rehabilitated (Appendix 5.4, Addendum 2 version)

This is a sanitary trunk sewer following Cooksville Creek, in precast or cast concrete pipe. Counted from the 40 segments in the repair summary:

The lined segments are circular and deeper than Toronto’s: upstream-manhole depths run from 3.72 m to 9.66 m.

Addendum 1 only released the CCTV videos (Appendices 5.24 and 5.25) to the prequalified contractors. Addendum 2 revised pay items, drawings and the repair summary, and moved the Phase 1 dates in Special Provisions 2.2, 3.1 and 21.3; Addendum 3 added flow data, relaxed the dates, and changed the thickness pay wording (Lesson 72).

What was in the folder. The main document, Addenda 1–3, the 2026 repair summary (both versions), Appendices 5.2 (both versions), 5.3, 5.9, 5.10, 5.13–5.21, 5.26, 5.28 and 5.34. Not in the folder: the online pricing forms (so the unit-price lines and the Domestic Supply Chain Plan form, Appendix 5.31, were not read), the Credit Valley Conservation permit (5.12, “to be included through Addendum”), the arborist report, flow data, as-builts and CCTV videos. One small drafting wrinkle: Addendum 3 quotes “January 31, 2027” as the text it replaces in Special Provision 80.1, although Addendum 2 had already changed that date to February 28.
