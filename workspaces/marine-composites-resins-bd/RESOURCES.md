# Marine Composites & Resins Resources

## Knowledge

- [The Gougeon Brothers on Boat Construction (West System / Gougeon Brothers, free PDF)](https://www.westsystem.com/app/uploads/2022/10/GougeonBook-061205-1.pdf)
  The primary reference for epoxy/wood-composite boat construction, from the company that has manufactured marine epoxy for 50+ years. Use for: epoxy fundamentals, laminate theory, structural design basics.
- [West System Fiberglass Boat Repair & Maintenance Manual (free PDF)](https://www.westsystem.com/app/uploads/2022/10/Fiberglass-Manual-2015.pdf)
  146-page manual covering gelcoat blisters, core repair, and laminate repair. Use for: understanding failure modes (blistering, delamination) that drive resin-selection arguments in sales conversations.
- [Epoxy Versus Polyester Resin for Fiberglass Boat Repair — Practical Sailor](https://www.practical-sailor.com/blog/epoxy-vs-polyester-resin-for-fiberglass-repair/)
  Independent marine-industry publication (not resin-manufacturer marketing). Use for: real-world tradeoffs and independent framing of epoxy vs. polyester.
- [Pros and Cons of Polyester, Vinylester & Epoxy Resins — Boats.net](https://www.boats.net/blog/marine-resins-guide-pros-cons)
  Practical buyer-facing comparison across all three resin families. Use for: quick side-by-side framing when building the resin comparison cheat sheet.
- [River Pools & Spas — Manufacturing with Vinyl Ester Resin](https://www.riverpoolsandspas.com/manufacturing-vinyl-ester-resin)
  Manufacturer explainer on why fiberglass pool shells use vinyl ester specifically (osmotic blister prevention). Use for: pool-segment resin rationale.
- [Leisure Pools — Full Vinyl Ester Resin Construction](https://leisurepoolsusa.com/technology/full-vinyl-ester-resin-construction)
  A second pool manufacturer's technical framing of vinyl ester vs. polyester shells. Use for: cross-checking the pool-segment story against more than one builder.
- [The Pros & Cons of Different Kayak Materials — Paddling Magazine](https://paddlingmag.com/boats/kayaks/what-are-kayaks-made-of/)
  Independent paddlesports publication comparing rotomolded polyethylene, composite (fiberglass/Kevlar/carbon + epoxy or polyester), and thermoform construction. Use for: kayak/canoe segment — note that a large share of this market uses no thermoset resin at all (rotomolded PE), which matters for targeting.
- [PWCToday — NanoXcel hulls and decks by Yamaha](https://www.pwctoday.com/forum/general-interest/general-pwc-stuff/78668-nanoxcel-hulls-and-decks-by-yamaha)
  Enthusiast/technical forum thread on Yamaha's SMC/compression-molded and NanoXcel hull materials. Use for: PWC segment — OEMs increasingly use proprietary molding compounds (SMC, NanoXcel, Polytec) rather than open resin/mat layup, which changes the sales angle vs. boats.
- [jetdrift.com — What is a Polytec Hull on a Sea-Doo?](https://www.jetdrift.com/sea-doo-polytec-hull/)
  Explains Sea-Doo's Polytec (glass-fiber/polypropylene blend) hull material. Use for: PWC segment — Sea-Doo has moved away from traditional polyester/fiberglass on many models.
- [ACMA — American Composites Manufacturers Association](https://acmanet.org/)
  Largest FRP composites industry trade association in North America; covers recreational boating among its member markets. Use for: industry standards, market data, and identifying trade events/technical committees relevant to BD outreach.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [Boat Design Net — Fiberglass & Composite Boat Building forum](https://www.boatdesign.net/forums/fiberglass-composite-boat-building/)
  Active, technically deep forum of boat builders and designers discussing resin selection, infusion, and repair. Use for: testing resin-pitch framing against people who actually spec materials, and for finding real objections before hearing them from a prospect.
- ACMA (see above) also runs industry conferences and technical committees — worth checking for a local/regional chapter or event once BD targeting narrows to a specific segment.
