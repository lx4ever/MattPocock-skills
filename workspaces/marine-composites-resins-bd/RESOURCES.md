# Marine Composites & Resins Resources

## Knowledge

- [The Gougeon Brothers on Boat Construction (West System / Gougeon Brothers, free PDF)](https://www.westsystem.com/app/uploads/2022/10/GougeonBook-061205-1.pdf)
  The primary reference for epoxy/wood-composite boat construction, from the company that has manufactured marine epoxy for 50+ years. Use for: epoxy fundamentals, laminate theory, structural design basics.
- [West System Fiberglass Boat Repair & Maintenance Manual (free PDF)](https://www.westsystem.com/app/uploads/2022/10/Fiberglass-Manual-2015.pdf)
  146-page manual covering gelcoat blisters, core repair, and laminate repair. Use for: understanding failure modes (blistering, delamination) that drive resin-selection arguments in sales conversations.
- [Epoxy Versus Polyester Resin for Fiberglass Boat Repair — Practical Sailor](https://www.practical-sailor.com/blog/epoxy-vs-polyester-resin-for-fiberglass-repair/)
  Independent marine-industry publication (not resin-manufacturer marketing). Use for: real-world tradeoffs and independent framing of epoxy vs. polyester.
- [Pros and Cons of Polyester, Vinylester & Epoxy Resins — Boats.net](https://www.boats.net/blog/marine-resins-guide-pros-cons)
  Practical buyer-facing comparison across all three resin families. Use for: quick side-by-side framing when building the resin comparison cheat sheet.
- [River Pools & Spas — Manufacturing with Vinyl Ester Resin](https://www.riverpoolsandspas.com/manufacturing-vinyl-ester-resin)
  Manufacturer explainer on why fiberglass pool shells use vinyl ester specifically (osmotic blister prevention). Use for: pool-segment resin rationale.
- [Leisure Pools — Full Vinyl Ester Resin Construction](https://leisurepoolsusa.com/technology/full-vinyl-ester-resin-construction)
  A second pool manufacturer's technical framing of vinyl ester vs. polyester shells. Use for: cross-checking the pool-segment story against more than one builder.
- [The Pros & Cons of Different Kayak Materials — Paddling Magazine](https://paddlingmag.com/boats/kayaks/what-are-kayaks-made-of/)
  Independent paddlesports publication comparing rotomolded polyethylene, composite (fiberglass/Kevlar/carbon + epoxy or polyester), and thermoform construction. Use for: kayak/canoe segment — note that a large share of this market uses no thermoset resin at all (rotomolded PE), which matters for targeting.
- [PWCToday — NanoXcel hulls and decks by Yamaha](https://www.pwctoday.com/forum/general-interest/general-pwc-stuff/78668-nanoxcel-hulls-and-decks-by-yamaha)
  Enthusiast/technical forum thread on Yamaha's SMC/compression-molded and NanoXcel hull materials. Use for: PWC segment — OEMs increasingly use proprietary molding compounds (SMC, NanoXcel, Polytec) rather than open resin/mat layup, which changes the sales angle vs. boats.
- [jetdrift.com — What is a Polytec Hull on a Sea-Doo?](https://www.jetdrift.com/sea-doo-polytec-hull/)
  Explains Sea-Doo's Polytec (glass-fiber/polypropylene blend) hull material. Use for: PWC segment — Sea-Doo has moved away from traditional polyester/fiberglass on many models.
- [ACMA — American Composites Manufacturers Association](https://acmanet.org/)
  Largest FRP composites industry trade association in North America; covers recreational boating among its member markets. Use for: industry standards, market data, and identifying trade events/technical committees relevant to BD outreach.
- [CompositesWorld — "Fabrication Methods"](https://www.compositesworld.com/articles/fabrication-methods)
  Trade-industry technical reference covering hand layup, vacuum infusion, RTM, and other composites processes side by side. Use for: boats segment — process fundamentals and how boat manufacturing compares to the wider composites industry.
- [Power & Motor Yacht — "Vacuum Resin Infusion Explained"](https://powerandmotoryacht.com/uncategorized/vacuum-resin-infusion-explained/)
  Industry-press explainer of vacuum infusion written for boat-industry readers rather than engineers. Use for: boats segment — the accessible primary source for the open-mold-vs-infusion contrast.
- [Wikipedia — "Resin transfer moulding"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resin_transfer_moulding)
  Baseline technical definition of RTM. Use for: boats segment — quick reference on the RTM process, cross-checked against CompositesWorld.
- [EPA — Boat Manufacturing: National Emission Standards for Hazardous Air Pollutants (NESHAP)](https://www.epa.gov/stationary-sources-air-pollution/boat-manufacturing-national-emission-standards-hazardous-air)
  Primary regulatory source — the actual federal standard governing styrene emissions from boat manufacturing. Use for: boats segment — citing the real compliance requirement in a sales conversation, not a secondhand summary of it.
- [EPA — Control Techniques Guidelines for Fiberglass Boat Manufacturing Materials (PDF)](https://www3.epa.gov/airquality/ctg_act/200809_voc_epa453_r-08-004_fiberglass_boat_manufacturing_materials.pdf)
  EPA's detailed technical guidance, including the specific low-VOC-resin and non-atomized-application emissions-reduction figures. Use for: boats segment — the numbers behind the "switch to lower-styrene resin" pitch.
- [Soundings Online — "Blister warranties becoming more common"](https://www.soundingsonline.com/news/blister-warranties-becoming-more-common)
  Boat-buyer-facing trade press piece on how blister warranty coverage and vinyl-ester/epoxy barrier coats have evolved. Use for: boats segment — the warranty-economics half of the buying-drivers story, written the way a builder's marketing team would frame it.
- [West System — "Gelcoat Blisters: Diagnosis, Repair and Prevention"](https://www.westsystem.com/app/uploads/2022/10/Gelcoat-Blisters-Diagnosis-Repair-and-Prevention.pdf)
  The field-standard reference on osmotic blistering mechanism, diagnosis, and repair — written by an epoxy manufacturer, so read the resin-choice conclusions with that lens, but the hydrolysis/osmosis chemistry explanation is solid. Use for: boats segment — the mechanism-level detail behind why polyester blisters and epoxy resists it.
- [Pettit Paint — Bulletin 1000: Gelcoat Blister Repair and Prevention (PDF)](https://www.pettitpaint.com/media/5858/pettit-bulletin-1000-gelcoat-blister-repair-prevention-may-2024.pdf)
  A bottom-paint manufacturer's practical guide to blister repair, including paint-compatibility considerations. Use for: boats segment — the repair-and-refinish side, complementing West System's chemistry focus.
- [Transport Canada — TP 1332, Construction Standards for Small Vessels (2010)](https://tc.canada.ca/en/marine-transportation/publications/tp-1332-construction-standards-small-vessels-2010)
  The Canadian federal construction standard for small vessels/pleasure craft, made under the Small Vessel Regulations (SOR/2010-91). Use for: boats segment, Canadian market — the baseline construction spec, including the flame-retardant-resin-in-engine-space requirement.
- [Transport Canada — Policy on acceptance of ABYC standards as an alternative to TP 1332](https://tc.canada.ca/en/marine-transportation/publications/marine-safety-management-system/tier-i-policies/tier-i-policy-acceptance-alternative-construction-requirements-small-vessels)
  Confirms Transport Canada accepts American Boat & Yacht Council standards in lieu of TP 1332 for pleasure craft <24m and non-pleasure craft ≤6m. Corroborated by a second search hit giving its formal title ("TIER I – Policy: Acceptance of Alternative Construction Requirements for Small Vessels"), effective date (Oct 30, 2019), and structure (Appendix 1, Table 1 = qualifying ABYC standards, Table 2 = required Canadian modifications). Use for: boats segment, Canadian market — explains why many "Canadian-spec" builds are really ABYC builds.
- [Canada Gazette / Justice Canada — Volatile Organic Compound Concentration Limits for Certain Products Regulations](https://gazette.gc.ca/rp-pr/p1/2019/2019-07-06/html/reg1-eng.html)
  Canada's federal VOC-limit regulatory framework for consumer/commercial products. Use for: boats segment, Canadian market — background only; not confirmed to cover boat-manufacturing resin specifically (see Gaps below).

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [Boat Design Net — Fiberglass & Composite Boat Building forum](https://www.boatdesign.net/forums/fiberglass-composite-boat-building/)
  Active, technically deep forum of boat builders and designers discussing resin selection, infusion, and repair. Use for: testing resin-pitch framing against people who actually spec materials, and for finding real objections before hearing them from a prospect.
- ACMA (see above) also runs industry conferences and technical committees — worth checking for a local/regional chapter or event once BD targeting narrows to a specific segment.
