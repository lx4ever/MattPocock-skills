# TP 1332 edition was wrong; flame-retardant claim upgraded to corroborated

Lesson 0004 had cited the 2010 edition of TP 1332 throughout. A user-supplied URL (path dated 2023-11) revealed Transport Canada has published editions through 2024, meaning the 2010 citation was stale — corrected in Lesson 0004, its cheat sheet, and RESOURCES.md. Separately, searching around that URL surfaced the flame-retardant-resin-in-engine-space claim a second time, verbatim, via an independent search path — upgrading it from "single unverified snippet" to "corroborated, likely accurate," though still not read from the primary PDF.

## Implications
- Future sessions should cite the newer TP1332E PDF (`https://tc.canada.ca/sites/default/files/2023-11/TP1332E.pdf`) as the primary reference, not the 2010 listing page — the old link is kept in RESOURCES.md only for traceability of the correction.
- The flame-retardant claim can now be raised as a well-supported question in a sales conversation, but still shouldn't be asserted with a section citation until the actual PDF is read.
- The single highest-value next step for this whole Canadian-standards thread is getting the actual TP1332E PDF read directly (user upload, since WebFetch is blocked entirely — see LR-0002) rather than more search-based triangulation.
