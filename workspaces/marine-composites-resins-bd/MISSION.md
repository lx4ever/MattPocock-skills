# Mission: Marine Composites & Resins for Resin BD

## Why
I'm doing business development to sell resins into marine recreational composite manufacturing — boats, PWCs (Sea-Doo/Yamaha-type), kayaks/canoes, and fiberglass pools. I need to understand the materials well enough to talk credibly with builders and plant buyers about resin choice, so I can identify the right prospects and pitch the right product for each one.

## Success looks like
- Can explain, unprompted, the practical differences between polyester, vinyl ester, and epoxy resin systems (cost, water/blister resistance, cure behavior, handling)
- Can name which resin/process each of the four segments (boats, PWC, kayak/canoe, pools) typically uses today, and why
- Can identify which segment(s) are the best fit for a given resin's strengths, and articulate the selling points a buyer in that segment actually cares about
- Can hold a credible technical conversation with a boat builder, PWC plant buyer, or pool manufacturer about resin trade-offs without being caught out on basics

## Constraints
- Starting point: comfortable with general chemistry/materials concepts, but no prior composites-specific background
- Still researching which specific resin/brand to represent — lessons should stay resin-agnostic and landscape-first until a product is chosen
- Covering all four segments (boats, PWC, kayak/canoe, pools) rather than narrowing to one first

## Out of scope
- Resin chemistry synthesis / how resin itself is manufactured upstream
- Non-marine composites industries (aerospace, automotive, wind)
- ABYC/regulatory certification paperwork, unless it becomes directly relevant to a sales conversation
