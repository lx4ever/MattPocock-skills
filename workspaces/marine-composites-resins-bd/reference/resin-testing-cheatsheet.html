Resin Testing & Certification Cheat Sheet

Tier 1 — resin manufacturer, before shipping (liquid resin)

Delivered per-lot as a Certificate of Analysis (COA).

Tier 2 — builder, after cure (finished laminate)

TestStandardCoverage
Barcol hardnessASTM D2583Can run on 100% of hulls — the production workhorse
Glass/resin content (ignition loss)ASTM D2584Structural QC, sampled
Void contentASTM D2734Sampled
Tensile / flexural / water absorptionASTM D638 / D790 / D570Design qualification, not per-hull

No such thing as a "boat-certified resin." There's no certifying body that stamps a resin as boat-approved. What governs a hull's laminate is the builder's engineering against a design standard (TP 1332, or a classification-society/ISO 12215 schedule), checked in production by Barcol and glass-content testing. If a buyer asks about "certification," walk through this two-tier picture — don't claim a certification that doesn't exist.
