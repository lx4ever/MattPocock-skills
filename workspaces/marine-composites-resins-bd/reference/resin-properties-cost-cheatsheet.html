Resin Properties & Cost Cheat Sheet

The compressed version of Lesson 0006.

PropertyBuilder's concernCost direction
ViscosityFlow/wet-out; infusion needs it low, vertical layup needs it controlledNeutral alone; costly combined with strength/water-resistance
Pot life / gel timeWorking time vs. production cycle speedFast-cure formulations cost more per gallon
ExothermCracking/blistering risk in thick laminatesControlled-exotherm formulations cost more
Tensile/flexural strength & elongationStructural load-bearing, impact resistanceEpoxy > vinyl ester > polyester, in both strength and price
Water absorption / hydrolytic stabilityBlister resistance (Lessons 0001/0005)~$45.50/gal polyester vs. ~$76.50/gal vinyl ester (illustrative snapshot, not a quote)
Flame retardancyRequired near engine spaces (Lesson 0004, corroborated not confirmed)Additive package adds cost and can trade off against other properties

Watch for apples-to-oranges cost comparisons. A real resin-vs-resin cost comparison holds reinforcement type, layup process, and labor constant. A number that looks like a clean resin comparison but silently changes the reinforcement too (e.g. chopped strand mat vs. woven cloth) will mislead you — one such comparison found epoxy cheaper than polyester per square foot, which contradicts every other pricing signal and is almost certainly an artifact of comparing two things at once.

Full citations in RESOURCES.md at the workspace root. Pricing is a search-snapshot, not a live quote — verify current pricing before using in a pitch.
