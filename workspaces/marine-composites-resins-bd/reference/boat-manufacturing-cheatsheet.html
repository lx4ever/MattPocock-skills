Boat Manufacturing Processes Cheat Sheet

The compressed version of Lesson 0002. Use this to read a plant floor in under a minute.

Process Mold type Resin properties that matter most Tells for spotting it Capital cost
Hand layup Open, single-sided Workable pot life, easy hand wet-out Rollers/brushes on the floor; visible resin spray/mist; low-volume or custom builds Lowest
Vacuum infusion Closed, vacuum bag over one-sided mold Low viscosity, controlled gel time, must fully wet the laminate before gelling Vacuum pumps, bagging film, flow-media mesh visible pre-pour; near-zero visible resin waste Moderate
Resin transfer molding (RTM) Closed, two-part (male + female) mold Similar to infusion, plus must tolerate injection pressure Heavy matched-tool molds; typically higher-volume or higher-precision production runs Highest

Selling angle by process. Hand-layup shops: resin is a compliance/cost lever (see Lesson 0003 on VOC regulation). Infusion/RTM shops: resin is a process-performance lever — flow time, gel time, and consistency batch-to-batch are what they'll grill you on.
