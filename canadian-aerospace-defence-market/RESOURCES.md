# Canadian Aerospace, Space and Defence Market Resources

## Knowledge

- [Defence and marine procurement — Public Services and Procurement Canada](https://www.canada.ca/en/public-services-procurement/services/acquisitions/defence-marine.html)
  PSPC's own portal for how it runs defence and marine buys. Use for: the mechanics of a specific competition, solicitation documents, and the National Shipbuilding Strategy project pages (e.g. River-class Destroyer).
- [Turning Complexity into Capability: Canada's Defence Procurement System for Major Projects — Office of the Procurement Ombud](https://opo-boa.gc.ca/tcc-eng.html)
  Independent, plain-language explainer of how DND, PSPC and ISED actually interact on a major project, written for people outside government. Use for: understanding the buyer-side process end to end before a pursuit.
- [Defence Capabilities Blueprint — Government of Canada](https://apps.forces.gc.ca/en/defence-capabilities-blueprint/index.asp)
  Searchable, official list of all 130+ major capital equipment, IT and infrastructure projects DND has funded, with timelines and budget ranges. Use for: finding out what's actually funded and roughly when it's expected to hit industry.
- [Industrial and Technological Benefits (ITB) Policy — ISED](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/industrial-technological-benefits/en/itb-policy)
  The official policy text: what triggers it ($100M+ non-trade-agreement defence/Coast Guard buys), what it obligates a winning prime to do in Canada.
- [Industrial and Technological Benefits Policy: Value Proposition Guide — ISED](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/industrial-technological-benefits/en/key-references/value-proposition-guide)
  How bidders' Value Propositions are scored and why a prime with a weaker price but stronger Canadian-content commitment can win. Use for: the exact mechanism that creates SME subcontracting demand — this is Kanata's real "in."
- [Planned Capital Spending under Canada's Defence Policy: 2025 Update — Parliamentary Budget Officer](https://www.pbo-dpb.ca/en/publications/RP-2526-016-S--planned-capital-spending-under-canada-defence-policy-2025-update--depenses-capital-prevues-titre-politique-defense-canada-mise-jour-2025)
  Independent, non-partisan costing and pacing of every major project against the $156.8B Capital Investment Fund (2024-25 to 2043-44). Use for: sanity-checking whether a program's budget/timeline claims from a prime or news article are realistic.
- [River-class destroyer (Canadian Surface Combatant) — PSPC project page](https://www.canada.ca/en/public-services-procurement/services/acquisitions/defence-marine/national-shipbuilding-strategy/projects/large-vessels/river-class-destroyer.html)
  Official status page for Canada's largest current defence program (~$77B, 15 hulls, Irving Shipbuilding, Type 26 design). Use for: CSC/River-class status checks — construction began mid-2026 on HMCS Fraser.
- [Canadian Multi-Mission Aircraft (CMMA / P-8A Poseidon) project — DND](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/defence/defence-equipment-purchases-upgrades/air-equipment-procurement/canadian-multi-mission-aircraft-project.html)
  Official page for the CP-140 Aurora replacement: up to 16 Boeing P-8A aircraft, ~$10.4B CAD, first delivery slipping toward 2027.
