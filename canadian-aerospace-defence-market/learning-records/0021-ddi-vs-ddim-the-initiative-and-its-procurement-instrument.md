# 0021: DDI is the policy initiative; DDIM is the specific procurement instrument — worth naming precisely

**From:** a direct correction to Lesson 16

## The finding

This workspace had been using "DDI" as shorthand for both the overall Defence Drone Initiative and the standing pre-qualified supplier marketplace it operates through — the thing with five streams, an RFSA, and a Technology Questionnaire. Precisely, that mechanism has its own name: the Defence Drone Initiative **Marketplace** (DDIM). DDI is the policy initiative; DDIM is the procurement instrument that implements it.

## Why it matters

"DDI" as loose shorthand is harmless in casual reference (and this workspace will keep using it that way in most places), but on an actual application or in a precise conversation with a DIA contact, using the correct instrument name (DDIM) signals the applicant actually understands the program's structure rather than having skimmed a news summary. Small naming precision like this compounds with the CC-330/STTC/A330-MRTT three-names lesson (13) and the CQ-9B Guardian/MQ-9B/RPAS naming layers (8, 15) — Canadian defence programs routinely have a policy name, an instrument name, and a public name, and knowing which is which reads as competence.
