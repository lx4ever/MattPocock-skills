# 0006: A large open ITB balance on a mostly-delivered fleet signals sustainment work, not build-to-print — and the automated pipeline score can under-rate it

**From:** Lesson 7 (AOPS)

## The finding

AOPS's RCN fleet is fully delivered (all 6 Harry DeWolf-class hulls in service since 2020–2025); only 2 Coast Guard hulls are still building. Kanata's tracker nonetheless shows a $2.02B "to be identified" ITB balance — the second-largest of any program in this workspace — against just one tracked Pipeline opportunity, scored 13 (the lowest seen so far). Both figures are accurate; they're describing different things. The open ITB balance is real but reflects the AJISS in-service-support contract (Thales, up to $800M CAD over an initial 8 years, extendable to 35, 170+ Canadian suppliers already engaged) — sustainment/modernization work — not new-hull construction. The Pipeline sheet's news-based scoring appears tuned to build-phase announcement language (keel layings, contract awards) and doesn't seem to register sustainment-contract activity the same way, hence the low score on a program with genuinely large open dollars.

Separately, Kanata's own tracker's note on this in-service contract ("$305M, 2012-2030") looks stale — the real, current AJISS contract (Sept 2023) is worth up to $800M over 8 years, a materially different and larger figure.

## Why it matters

Extends the running theme (Lessons 2, 3, 5, 6) one step further: it's not enough to check *whether* an ITB balance is current — the *type* of remaining obligation has to be matched against the program's actual delivery stage. A big balance on a nearly-finished build program means sustainment/modernization opportunity, and a low automated pipeline score on that kind of program is a cue to check manually, not a verdict that the program is unpromising.

## Follow-up

Worth spot-checking whether other "mostly delivered" programs in Kanata's list (if any) show the same score-vs-balance mismatch, and flagging the stale $305M AJISS figure back for an update to the real $800M number.
