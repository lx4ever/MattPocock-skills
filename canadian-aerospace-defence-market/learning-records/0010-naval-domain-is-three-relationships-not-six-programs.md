# 0010: The naval domain reduces to three relationships (Irving, Seaspan, Thales), not six separate program pursuits

**From:** Lesson 11 (Joint Support Ship)

## The finding

Lesson 7 found AOPS's real in-service-support contract (Thales's AJISS) also covers JSS — the "A" and "JSS" are both in the contract's own name. Combined with the named contacts already in Kanata's tracker, the naval domain shows the same hub-and-spoke shape Lesson 5 found in space: Irving Shipbuilding (contact: Aaron Plamondon) is the entry point for both River-class Destroyer and AOPS; Seaspan Vancouver Shipyards (contact: Chad Hey) is the entry point for JSS, Multi-Purpose Icebreakers, and NG-PCPV; Thales cross-cuts both build primes as the shared sustainment layer via AJISS. Six curated naval programs reduce to three real relationships.

## Why it matters

Same practical implication as Lesson 5's MDA finding: a BD plan organized program-by-program (six separate outreach efforts) misses that three conversations — with Irving, Seaspan, and Thales — already reach the whole domain. Worth checking whether this three-relationship structure is reflected in how Kanata actually sequences naval outreach, or whether it's still being planned per-program.

## Follow-up

Also confirms a broader pattern across four lessons now: naval/space domains concentrate around a small number of prime relationships (MDA in space; Irving/Seaspan/Thales in naval), while Land (Lesson 9) showed a similar concentration (General Dynamics, Rheinmetall) despite having no curated programs at all. Worth checking whether Air and Training show the same shape once fully mapped.
