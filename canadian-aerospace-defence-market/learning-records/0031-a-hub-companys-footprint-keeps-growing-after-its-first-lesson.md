# 0031: A hub company's real footprint keeps growing well after its first lesson

**From:** Lesson 29 (Thales Canada)

## The finding

Thales entered this workspace in Lesson 3 as one line in a program team list, became a named naval hub in Lesson 7 (AJISS), got folded into a three-hub naval summary in Lesson 11, and was flagged as an EW watch-list candidate in Lesson 21. Four separate touches, each adding one fact, none of which added up to "who is Thales Canada, actually." Revisiting the company directly surfaced: its Canadian HQ is specifically in the Kanata district of Ottawa (not just "the same city," the same neighbourhood as Kanata Electronics); a second, separate 2026 contract (S2087 sonar, via Lockheed Martin Canada) on the same ship program AJISS already covers; and an entirely new program, NVSM, with its own dedicated manufacturing site, that hadn't appeared in this workspace at all.

## Why it matters

This is the same shape as Lesson 27's GDMS-Canada finding (a company's fuller picture only appears once someone looks at the company, not just the program it was first attached to) but with a different mechanism: GDMS-Canada was under-described because one contract family was wrongly read as the whole company; Thales Canada was under-described because it kept appearing in small fragments across five lessons without anyone ever asking "what else does this company do." A hub worth naming once is worth revisiting directly, not just accumulating incidental mentions of.

## Follow-up

Check whether any other multi-lesson recurring name in this workspace (L3Harris, MDA, Irving, Seaspan) has the same fragmentation problem — each has appeared in several lessons but hasn't had its own dedicated "who is this company, fully" lesson the way GDMS-Canada and Thales Canada now have.
