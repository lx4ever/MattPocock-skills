# 0017: Some doors are capital, not customers — and Kanata is confirmed inside one of them

**From:** Lesson 18 (DIA, Strix/INSAT & FedDev)

## The finding

Every source covered before this lesson (Lessons 1–17) was either a buyer (DND/PSPC/a prime) or a body enforcing/registering a Canadian-content obligation (ISED/ITB). This lesson surfaced two sources that are neither: Strix/INSAT co-funds aerospace R&D projects (including dual-use technology), and FedDev Ontario's Regional Defence Investment Initiative (RDII) directly grants money to SMEs to build defence-sector capability. Neither pays for a delivered product — both provide capital to get ready to deliver one. FedDev Ontario's mandate area was confirmed to include Ottawa directly (one of its four regional offices), so this isn't a stretch-eligibility case for Kanata.

Also resolved: the Defence Investment Agency (DIA), established October 2025, has been the actual issuing body behind several announcements already cited in this workspace (SofS2, CC-330's ISS contracts, the CPSP backgrounder) without ever being explained — it's centralizing much of what Lesson 1 attributed to PSPC. And "INSED" doesn't exist as a distinct entity; it's almost certainly INSAT, Strix's program.

## Why it matters

The workspace's pursuit strategy has been entirely "win a subcontract from X." Capability-funding sources like FedDev's RDII change the sequencing question: instead of only asking "how do we win work that requires MOSA/ICD documentation or DDI qualification," it's worth asking "can we get funded to build that capability first, then bid." Given DDI's near-term deadline (Lesson 16) and MOSA's concrete documentation requirements (Lesson 16's learning record), RDII is a plausible, directly-eligible funding source for exactly that gap.
