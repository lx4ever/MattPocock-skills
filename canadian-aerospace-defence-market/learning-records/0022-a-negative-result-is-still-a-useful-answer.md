# 0022: "No confirmed prime yet, no 49North role found" is still a useful, actionable answer

**From:** follow-up research on Lesson 21 (C4ISR)

## The finding

Neither Tactical Communications Modernization nor Land Electronic Warfare Modernization has a publicly named prime contractor — both are still pre-contract (TCM budgeted at ~$690M under the wider LCSS Modernization effort; LEWM has a Defence Capabilities Blueprint entry with no contractor attached, the only nearby contract action being a facilities design award for 21 Electronic Warfare Regiment's new building, not the equipment itself). No source ties 49North to either project.

Rather than a dead end, this reframes the question correctly: it's not "which prime do we approach" (there isn't one yet), it's "who's already positioned, worth watching for the eventual award." Two companies already inside this workspace's relationship map — L3Harris (existing CAF tactical radio contract, already Kanata's contact for F-35 and CC-330) and Thales (a stated communications/EW product line, already the AJISS naval hub) — are plausible incumbent-adjacent candidates, without being confirmed as anything yet.

## Why it matters

A "no" to the literal question asked ("who primes this, does 49North have a role") isn't the same as "nothing useful here." The honest negative result — pre-contract, no confirmed prime, no 49North tie — is itself the actionable fact: this is a watch-and-wait situation, not a pursuit-now situation, and the useful next step is identifying who's plausibly positioned rather than forcing a premature answer to a question the public record doesn't yet support.

## Follow-up

Re-check both projects periodically for a contract award announcement — at that point this moves from "watch" to an actual pursuit lesson, the same way CPSP (Lesson 4) moved from pre-contract positioning to a named preferred supplier.
