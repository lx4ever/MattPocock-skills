# 0004: Space concentrates around one relationship (MDA); naval/air don't

**From:** Lesson 5 (Space Domain / MDA Hub)

## The finding

Across the three programs covered before this one (CMMA/Boeing, River-class Destroyer/Irving, CPSP/TKMS), each program's supplier-registration relationship was separate — one prime, one portal, one contact, no overlap. The space domain doesn't work that way: MDA Space is the prime or named key player on 5 of Kanata's 10 curated space programs (Canadarm3, RADARSAT, RADARSAT NG concept study, Telesat Lightspeed, SofS2). One relationship — MDA's own supply-chain vendor questionnaire — is a door into most of the domain simultaneously, backed by a concrete number: the $1B Canadarm3 Phase C/D contract alone involves over 200 Canadian companies in MDA's supply chain.

Kepler Communications is a smaller, second version of the same pattern — appearing across three curated programs (prime on one already-flying constellation, positioning on two pre-award ones).

## Why it matters

This changes the shape of a BD strategy for the space domain versus naval/air: instead of researching and registering with a different prime per program, the highest-leverage single action is getting into MDA's supply chain once, since it's reused across the majority of the curated list. Worth checking whether this "hub" pattern holds (or doesn't) in the remaining domains — training (SkyAlyne on FAcT) and land — before assuming it's universal.
