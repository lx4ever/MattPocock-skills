# 0024: A program can be the right stage and the right sector and still be the wrong moment — check the current wave, not just the eligibility criteria

**From:** Lesson 23 (CDIR, CDDE & NADCAP)

## The finding

CDIR is structurally the right program for Kanata: DND-run, TRL 9+/market-and-production stage (confirmed in Lesson 22), and its named priority sectors explicitly include "dual-use manufacturing." But CDIR doesn't run as an open rolling application — it funds through targeted Calls for Proposals tied to whatever DND's current sovereign-production priority is, and that priority right now is ammunition and explosives production, tied to a separate $1.4B investment announced in March 2026. Meeting the eligibility criteria on paper doesn't mean this is the moment to apply — the current CFP wave doesn't match Kanata's sector at all, even though a future one plausibly could.

## Why it matters

Every prior "does this fit" assessment in this workspace (Lessons 10, 21) checked sector/capability match. This is a different, additional axis: timing. A program can be a perfect stage-and-sector match in its general eligibility language while its actual current funding round is aimed somewhere else entirely. The right move isn't to skip CDIR — it's to treat the mismatch as a reason to consult early (ask DND directly when a maritime/dual-use CFP might open) rather than either applying against the wrong wave or writing the program off as irrelevant.

## Follow-up

Check back on CDIR's active CFPs periodically, or ask directly in the recommended consultation, for whether a dual-use-manufacturing or maritime-systems wave is anticipated.
