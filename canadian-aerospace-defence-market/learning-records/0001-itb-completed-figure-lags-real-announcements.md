# 0001: ISED's "completed to date" figure lags real, public ITB announcements

**From:** Lesson 2 (CMMA / P-8A Poseidon)

## The finding

Kanata's own ITB obligations tracker shows CMMA's "completed to date" as **USD $0**, even though Boeing publicly announced a real, dated $61M ITB investment in BC aerospace back in August 2024 ($48M into its own Vancouver R&D facility, $13M into Indigenous-owned COTA Aviation).

## Why it matters

"$0 completed" on the ISED register is not the same claim as "nothing has happened yet." The register's completed/in-progress/to-be-identified split is an accounting/reporting category that lags behind — and is a separate process from — a prime's own press releases. When sizing up how "hot" a program's ITB obligation actually is, or how credible a prime's Canadian-investment story is, check the prime's own announcements as well as the ISED figures. Relying on ISED's "completed" column alone would have made this program look untouched when it isn't.

## Follow-up

Worth checking whether this pattern holds on other programs in Kanata's tracker (AOPS, JSS, River-class) before treating any "$0 completed" figure as a signal that a prime hasn't started delivering on its Value Proposition.
