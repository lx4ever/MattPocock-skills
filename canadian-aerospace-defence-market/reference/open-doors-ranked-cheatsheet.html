Open Doors, Ranked

Lessons 16–24 surfaced a second category beyond program-level ITB pursuits: applications, funding, and networks Kanata could act on directly. They are not equally open. Ranked by how actionable each one is right now.

#DoorStatusWhy this rank
1Team SkyGuardian CanadaOpen, standing application — ga-asi.com/TeamSkyGuardianCanadaNo deadline, no competition described in the public record, explicitly recruiting SMEs, a named fit category ("global supply chain for aircraft components and manufacturing"), Ottawa office. The closest thing to "just apply."
2DDIM (Defence Drone Initiative Marketplace)Open but deadline August 14, 2026Competitive pre-qualification, not guaranteed, but genuinely open to SMEs across a broad Stream 1 (UAS/UGV/USV/UUV). Time-boxed — act or miss the window.
3MDA Space vendor questionnaire (Canadarm3)Open, standing, named contact$916.5M USD still unplaced, real pipeline entry already scored Reach Out, contact already identified (Francois Provencher).
4NADCAP accreditation (Electronics: cable/harness/soldering)Actionable investment, not an applicationPriced ($15K–$60K + $10K–$75K consulting, $3–5K/yr) and timed (6–18mo). Strengthens the case for #1 and #2 simultaneously, plus MDA's stated requirement.
5FedDev Ontario / RDIIOpen, direct grant — allocation/deadline not yet confirmed preciselyKanata's Ottawa location is confirmed inside the mandate area and RDII sits at exactly Kanata's TRL (9+, market/production). Needs one direct call to FedDev to firm up the number before citing it.
6CDIR consultationReal money ($25M–$642M+), wrong current waveStructurally the right stage, but the active Call for Proposals is ammunition/explosives, not Kanata's sector. Worth a consultation (CDIR-RIDC@forces.gc.ca) about a future maritime/dual-use wave, not an application against this one.
7Maritime DISH (Halifax — autonomous underwater systems, maritime sensing)Exists; engagement process unconfirmedThe closest capability match to Kanata's recommended USV/UUV positioning of anything in this list — but no public partner-engagement process found yet, unlike Team SkyGuardian's clear application.
8Strix / INSATR&D co-funding, needs a project to pitchNot a "register interest" door — only useful once Kanata has a specific technology or process (e.g. MOSA-compliant harness documentation) worth developing collaboratively.
9CDDE (TKMS / CPSP)Indirect — university-mediated15-year research framework via UBC/Western/Dalhousie, not a direct company application. Realistic path is through a partner university's specific project.
10Tactical Comms Mod / Land EW ModPre-contract, no prime yetWatch-and-wait, not pursuit — re-rank once either project reaches contract award (see Hubs & Contacts for the incumbent-adjacent candidates to watch).

If only doing one thing this month: Team SkyGuardian Canada's application, given DDIM's clock is also running — the two aren't mutually exclusive and both draw on the same underlying capability pitch.
