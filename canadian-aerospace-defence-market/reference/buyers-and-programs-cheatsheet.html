Who buys

BodyControls
DND (Department of National Defence)The requirement. Defines what capability is needed and why.
PSPC (Public Services and Procurement Canada)The contract. Runs the competition, signs the deal — the only body with authority to actually buy defence goods on DND's behalf.
ISED (Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada)The Canadian-content obligation. Owns the ITB Policy and scores each bidder's Value Proposition.

The mechanism that matters for Kanata

ITB Value Proposition: on defence/Coast Guard buys over $100M (outside trade agreements), the winning prime is contractually obligated to do business in Canada equal to the contract's value — scored as part of the bid, not offered voluntarily. That obligation is what forces a prime down into subcontracts, including to SME suppliers. This is the lever, not a relationship favour.
