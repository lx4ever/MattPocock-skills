Lesson 20 · Canadian Aerospace, Space and Defence Market

UxS DISH / NEXUS

Not another program to apply to — the door here is already closed. But one named member's stated focus lines up closely enough with Kanata's capability that it's worth tracking as a future subcontracting lead.

What it is

FactDetail
Full nameUncrewed Systems Defence Innovation Secure Hub (UxS DISH), code-named NEXUS
Parent programRuns under BOREALIS, Canada's defence innovation accelerator
AnnouncedJuly 14, 2026, by Defence Minister David McGuinty
LocationYMX Innovation Centre, Mirabel, Quebec — secure collaboration space, specialized testing infrastructure, and access to regulated airspace
SponsorCanadian Joint Forces Command (CJFC)
Funding$29.6M over two years
Consortium30 organizations, led by Aéro Montréal via Espace Aéro

It's a secure environment where government, CAF, industry, and researchers develop, test, validate, and integrate emerging uncrewed and autonomous systems technologies — structured around five operational use cases covering counter-uncrewed systems and counter-robotics/autonomous systems specifically, not the full breadth of DDI's Stream 1.

Sources: DND, July 2026; Octasic newsroom.

Why this isn't an application to make

Unlike DDI (Lesson 16), there's no standing, rolling application here. The consortium was selected through a competitive Call for Proposals held February 18 – April 2, 2026 — already closed. The 30 selected organizations are the hub's primary users; DND's own framing says other companies "will likely be brought in as collaborators" depending on what specific projects the consortium undertakes, but that's a project-by-project pull, not something Kanata can apply for directly right now.

The one lead worth tracking

Octasic, a named consortium member (joined July 15, 2026), contributes advanced RF technologies, software-defined radio (SDR) systems, and mission payload development, specifically to advance spectrum sensing and counter-drone (C-UAS) capabilities. RF/SDR hardware needs precision cabling, shielding, and connector work — a closer capability match to Kanata's harness/electrical-assembly work than most of what else NEXUS covers. If a real entry point into this hub exists for Kanata, it's more likely through a project-level relationship with a member like Octasic than through the hub itself.

Primary source to read next Octasic's own announcement — the most specific, named starting point if this is worth pursuing at all.
