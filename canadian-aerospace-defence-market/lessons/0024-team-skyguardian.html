Lesson 24 · Canadian Aerospace, Space and Defence Market

Team SkyGuardian Canada

Lessons 8 and 15 treated this as background — the industry team behind RPAS. It's actually one of the most directly actionable doors in this whole workspace: a named, open, SME-targeted application, with an office a short drive from Kanata.

What it is, and who's in it

Team SkyGuardian Canada (TSC) is General Atomics Aeronautical Systems' (GA-ASI) industry coalition supporting Canada's MQ-9B/CQ-9B Guardian RPAS program (Lessons 8, 15, 17). Four named members, each with a distinct role:

MemberRole
GA-ASILeads the partnership on CQ-9B development, delivery, and sustainment
CAE CanadaAircrew training and in-service support for the RCAF, plus training solutions across GA-ASI's RPAS family
MDA / 49NorthPrimary supplier of unmanned systems infrastructure — Ground Control Stations, the Coalition Shared Database (Lesson 15)
L3Harris Technologies (WESCAM)Sensor/EO systems contribution to the coalition

The scale most lessons haven't captured

This isn't a four-company team — it's a network. Over 2,000 Canadian contractors and suppliers are already working with Team SkyGuardian, and GA-ASI states it's actively growing that number, "particularly" seeking small and medium sized businesses. GA-ASI names its areas of interest for Canadian expertise directly: aircraft operations and maintenance; sensor data processing, exploitation and dissemination; airborne sensors/payloads; global supply chain for aircraft components and manufacturing; and UAS-related R&D. That fourth category is Kanata's exact fit.

The application is real, open, and named

Unlike DDI (competitive, deadline-driven, Lesson 16), CDIR (right stage, wrong current wave, Lesson 23), or CDDE (indirect, university-mediated, Lesson 23), Team SkyGuardian Canada runs a standing, direct application: ga-asi.com/TeamSkyGuardianCanada. Canadian businesses with aerospace capabilities, remotely piloted systems, or autonomous technologies apply to join GA-ASI, MDA, L3Harris, and CAE Canada directly — no consortium selection window, no competitive scoring against other applicants described in the public record.

GA-ASI's Canadian office for this program is located near Confederation Park, Ottawa — the same city as Kanata, and closer than any other program-specific office covered in this workspace besides 49North and the C4ISR & Beyond conference (Lesson 21).

Sources: General Atomics, "Team SkyGuardian Canada Looking to Grow"; Air Force Technology, on the Ottawa office.

Where this sits against everything else covered

Team SkyGuardian doesn't fix that gap in Kanata's tracker, but it does mean the practical next step doesn't wait on that fix — the application is open right now, aimed explicitly at companies like Kanata, through a local office.
