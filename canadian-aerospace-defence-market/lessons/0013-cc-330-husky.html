Lesson 13 · Canadian Aerospace, Space and Defence Market

CC-330 Husky

Three names for one program, and a contract note in Kanata's tracker whose own end date has already passed.

Same program, three names

NameUsed by
CC-330 HuskyRCAF's aircraft designation — what you'll see in news coverage
Strategic Tanker Transport Capability (STTC)The procurement project's official name — what ISED's ITB register uses
Airbus A330 MRTTThe manufacturer's product name — what Airbus's own materials use

Worth knowing all three: searching only "CC-330 Husky" would miss the ITB register entirely, since it's filed under "Strategic Tanker Transport Capability." The same naming-layer pattern showed up with CMMA/P-8A (Lesson 2) and River-class Destroyer/CSC (Lesson 3) — procurement project names and public aircraft/ship names diverge more often than not.

What's actually being bought

The CC-330 Husky replaces the CC-150 Polaris, providing strategic airlift, air-to-air refuelling, aeromedical evacuation, and government transport. Fleet acquisition and conversion runs through roughly 2028, prime contractor Airbus Defence and Space.

The stale contract note — caught by its own end date

Kanata's tracker cites "L3Harris MAS - STTC Interim In-Service Support ($70.4M, 2022-2025)." That contract's own stated end date — 2025 — has already passed. Unlike previous staleness catches in this workspace, which needed cross-referencing against other sources, this one is visible from the note itself: an "Interim" contract whose end date is behind today's date is a contract that's already been superseded by something. In this case: in March 2026, Canada's Defence Investment Agency awarded L3Harris MAS two long-term contracts — a 10-year maintenance agreement and a 7-year materiel-support agreement, both with options to extend to 20 years, worth roughly $1.1B CAD combined — plus a third contract to Airbus Defence and Space itself for OEM support services. The full sustainment package is valued around $1.5B CAD. The L3Harris MAS piece alone is projected to contribute close to $70M in GDP and support 550+ jobs annually.

Source: Defence Investment Agency, March 2026.

Where this leaves Kanata

Kanata's tracker has 5 tracked opportunities on this program (4 Review, 1 Reach Out), top score 23, with named contacts Mark Schmidt (Airbus D&S) and Robert van den Heuvel (L3Harris MAS). Van den Heuvel's contact record is tied to the now-superseded Interim contract in the tracker's notes — worth confirming he's still the right named contact under the new long-term agreements before leading an outreach with a citation to a contract that no longer exists, rather than assuming the note is still accurate.

Primary source to read next The Defence Investment Agency's March 2026 announcement — the direct replacement for the stale Interim-contract note, and the clearest picture of what CC-330's real, current sustainment structure looks like.
